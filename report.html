🌐 External Search Findings ▼

GPOMCT Grupo Empresarial S.A. de C.V. operates as an international, Mexico-based wholesale currency dealer and currency exchange business, controlling over 40 locations in Mexico [1]. The company pleaded guilty to operating an unlicensed money transmitting business in the United States and was cited for anti-money-laundering deficiencies [1] [6] [7]. GPOMCT used the U.S. financial system to process large amounts of currency, some of which were at high risk for being criminal proceeds, without proper registration or compliance with U.S. AML regulations [1]. Between 2019 and 2021, GPOMCT imported large volumes of U.S. dollars into the United States, including approximately 195 shipments of currency (each worth between $90,000 and $100,000 USD) delivered to a currency exchange in San Ysidro, California [1] [2] [4] [5]. The company was ordered to forfeit $1.1 million and sentenced to three years' probation [1]. There is also a mention of a civil proceeding in the Baja California Judicial Bulletin involving GPOMCT Grupo Empresarial related to a mortgaged property [9].
